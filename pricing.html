Questions

Before you ask

Do we need to buy new hardware?

No. Nexulith runs on the Android or iOS phones your supervisors already carry, and works with most existing barcode scanners. If you already label products, you are ready to scan.

What if the network drops on the floor?

Entries are queued on the device and synced automatically when the connection returns. Operators keep working; nothing is lost and no one has to remember what happened hours later.

Can the software match our existing forms?

That is the point. We model your fields, units, shifts, approval steps and exceptions during onboarding, so the screen matches how your team already records production.

Who owns the data?

You do. Your production data stays yours and can be exported at any time — as CSV or through the API — even if you stop using Nexulith.

How long does it take to go live?

Most pilots are live within 7 to 14 days, including setup, field mapping and operator training. Complex multi-line deployments are scoped individually.
