01 — How we help

Three ways we put your floor on record

Every factory records differently. Choose the entry method that fits the line — we build the rest of the software around it, not the other way round.

01

QR & barcode entry

For factories that already label cartons, pallets or parts: operators scan once and Nexulith records product, batch, shift and timestamp instantly. No spreadsheets, no double entry, no backlog waiting for the end of the shift.

02

Photo-based capture

For legacy parts and handwritten labels with nothing to scan: your team takes one photo and the software reads the serial, quantity and spec. We help you digitise years of uncoded production without re-labelling a single line.

Custom entry workflows

For plants where every line reports differently: we model your exact fields, units, approval steps and exceptions, then give each shift a single screen that matches how they already work — so adoption takes days, not months.
